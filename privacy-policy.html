Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026  |  Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who we are

Elevenfold (the "Game") is published by Keya Games Inc. ("Keya Games", "we", "us", "our"), 1401 Pennsylvania Ave., Suite 105, #2334, Wilmington, Delaware 19806, USA. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game on iOS or Android, why, and the choices you have. Questions: support@keyagames.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts, no login and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information.

2.2 Information stored on your device

Your game progress (board, score, best score, level, power-ups and settings) is saved only on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2.3 Information created automatically when you play

The third-party services described in Section 5 receive, from your device:

  • advertising identifiers: on iOS the IDFA (only if you allow tracking), on Android the Google Advertising ID;
  • a randomly generated analytics identifier;
  • device information such as model, operating system and version, language and screen size;
  • IP address and the approximate (country/region-level) location derived from it;
  • gameplay events (for example sessions, progress, game overs and feature use) and crash information;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with.

3. How we use information

  • to show ads that keep the Game free, including optional rewarded ads (such as the revive);
  • to measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud;
  • to understand how the Game is played and improve its balance, design and stability.

4. Advertising and your choices

4.1 Advertising

Ads are served through AppLovin MAX and AppLovin's advertising partners. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised using your advertising identifier and device information.

4.2 Your choices

  • iOS: the Game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your IDFA can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (wording varies by device).
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent to personalised advertising through Google's User Messaging Platform; you can withdraw it through the same consent message.
  • If you decline tracking or personalisation you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

5. Who we share information with

The following providers process the information described above under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin (MAX) — advertising and ad measurement — applovin.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics — gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Google (User Messaging Platform) — consent management in the EEA, UK and Switzerland — policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing your advertising identifier with advertising partners so they can serve or measure personalised ads may, however, count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some U.S. state privacy laws. Section 7 explains how to opt out. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, or to a successor in a merger or acquisition.

6. Data retention

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. Third-party providers keep the information they receive for the periods described in their privacy policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

EEA/UK (GDPR): our legal bases are legitimate interests (analytics, contextual advertising, fraud prevention) and consent (personalised advertising and tracking).

United States: depending on your state of residence (including California under the CCPA/CPRA, and residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, Montana, and other states with comprehensive privacy laws), you may have the right to know/access, delete, and correct your personal information, to opt out of targeted advertising and the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. To opt out of the use of your advertising identifier for personalised advertising, decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS or opt out of ads personalisation on Android as described in Section 4.2. You may also email an opt-out request to us.

Because we do not hold an account or a profile about you, most information sits with the providers in Section 5; to exercise a right, contact us at support@keyagames.com and we will help you, including by passing your request to the relevant provider.

8. International data transfers

Keya Games Inc. is a company incorporated in the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards described in their privacy policies (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

9. Children

Elevenfold is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Game, contact us at support@keyagames.com and we will help have it deleted.

10. Security

We choose providers that use industry-standard measures to protect the information they process. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be reflected in the Game's store listings.
